# Survival Cinema

**Play. It makes the movie.**

Survival Cinema records your survival in the background and turns the best moments into a real film: your first diamonds, the night you nearly died, the creeper that blew up your house, the moment everyone started yelling on voice chat, the dragon. Cinematic cameras, chapters, music, a timelapse of your base and end credits — edited by itself and played in a little cinema right inside Minecraft. It's a normal MP4 you can share.

## How it works

1. **Play** the way you always do. A small ● REC sits in the corner.
2. **Kill the dragon** — or press the clapperboard in the pause menu any time.
3. **Pick 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes** and press *Start shooting*.
4. **Watch.** The film plays by itself when it's ready.

Got friends on Simple Voice Chat or a Discord call? Their voices go into the film, and the loudest moments get the spotlight.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.11**, Fabric Loader, Fabric API
- **[Flashback](https://modrinth.com/mod/flashback)** — the replay mod Survival Cinema films with
- Optional: [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-voice-chat), [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu)

Client-side only, works on any server. Everything stays on your computer.
