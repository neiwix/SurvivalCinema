# Changelog

## 1.0.0-beta.1 (Minecraft 1.21.11)

The first public version.

- Records your survival with Flashback in the background and notices the moments that matter.
- Turns them into a 5–20 minute film: cinematic cameras, chapters, music, a timelapse of your base, end credits.
- Voices from Simple Voice Chat, and on Windows from Discord and other calls.
- The film is offered after the Ender Dragon, or any time from the pause menu, and plays in an in-game cinema.
- English and Russian.
