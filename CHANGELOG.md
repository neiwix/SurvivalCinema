# Changelog

## 1.0.0-beta.1 (Minecraft 1.21.11)

The first public version.

- Records your survival with Flashback in the background: asks once per world, pauses when you are AFK, a small ● REC sign while it runs.
- Notices the moments that matter: advancements, new dimensions, boss fights and the Ender Dragon, deaths and near-deaths, kill streaks, rare finds, building, elytra flights, big falls, nights and sleep — and the moments when people shout or talk.
- Plans a 5, 10, 15 or 20 minute film: voice plus an event first, then the big story beats, the whole playthrough covered, never padded with filler.
- Shoots it with cinematic cameras (orbit, side, pull-back, two-shot, slow motion) that follow your recorded path and keep out of walls and trees, under its own "Shooting the film…" screen. Voices stay at normal speed over slow motion. Stop with Esc and continue later, even after a crash.
- Edits one MP4: chapter cards in Minecraft's font, Minecraft's soundtrack or your own music (ducked under voices and loud moments), a timelapse of your base, end credits with your world's stats.
- Plays the film in an in-game cinema (pause, seek, volume) as soon as it is ready.
- Offers to make the film after the Ender Dragon; any time from the pause menu.
- Voices: Simple Voice Chat, and on Windows calls in Discord, Telegram, TeamSpeak, Zoom, WhatsApp and Skype plus your microphone.
- Settings screen (also from Mod Menu), choice of which recordings go into the film, disk space screen with clean-up, optional deletion of the recordings once a film is made.
- Checks the installed Flashback at startup and turns itself off with a clear message instead of crashing when a Flashback update changed something it uses.
- English and Russian.
